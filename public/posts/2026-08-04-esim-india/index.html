The Ultimate Indian Traveler’s Guide to eSIMs, Dual SIM Setups, and International Travel

Navigating foreign connectivity while keeping your primary home numbers active can feel like a complex puzzle. Between Jio, Airtel, local international SIMs, physical SIM slots, and digital eSIM profiles, it is easy to get confused about what stays on, what gets turned off, and what actually needs to be physically swapped.

This complete guide breaks down everything you need to know about switching your Indian mobile lines (Jio and Airtel) to eSIM, configuring dual SIM settings for overseas travel, and managing a 3-SIM scenario on a single smartphone.

1. How to Convert Your Jio or Airtel SIM to an eSIM#

Converting your physical SIM card to an eSIM frees up your physical SIM slot—an essential step if you plan to plug in a local prepaid physical SIM when traveling abroad.

Activating a Jio eSIM#

  1. Check Compatibility & Grab Details: Dial *#06# on your phone to retrieve your 32-digit EID and 15-digit IMEI numbers.
  2. Send Request SMS: Send an SMS from your Jio number to 199:

GETESIM <32-digit EID> <15-digit IMEI>
  1. Confirm the Request: You will receive a 19-digit eSIM number via SMS. Reply with 1 to 199 to confirm.
  2. Automated Call Verification: You will receive an automated call on your Jio number. Follow the IVR instructions to give your formal consent.
  3. Install Profile: Open your phone’s Settings > Mobile Data / Cellular > Add Data Plan / Add eSIM. Your Jio eSIM profile will download automatically, or you can scan the QR code sent to your registered email.

Activating an Airtel eSIM#

  1. Initiate Request: Open the Airtel Thanks App on your device.
  2. Select eSIM Section: Tap on your profile / primary mobile number section and select Upgrade to eSIM.
  3. Identity Verification: Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number and email.
  4. SMS Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation SMS from 121. Reply with 1 within 60 seconds to confirm the conversion.
  5. Finalizing Profile Setup: A push notification or prompt will appear on your device to install the cellular profile. Follow the on-screen instructions under your network settings to complete activation.

Important Notes During Activation:

  • Keep your device connected to a stable Wi-Fi network throughout the download.
  • Your old physical SIM will stop working once the eSIM activates (usually within 1 to 2 hours).
  • Per government regulations, SMS services (incoming and outgoing) remain blocked for 24 hours after eSIM activation to prevent fraud.

2. Using a Physical SIM + eSIM for International Travel#

Once your Indian primary line (e.g., Jio) is on an eSIM, your phone’s physical SIM tray is empty. When traveling to a foreign country, you can insert a local physical SIM card to get cheap data, local calls, and fast connectivity while keeping your home line active for incoming OTPs.

Steps to Configure Dual SIM Abroad#

  1. Insert International SIM: Put the local physical SIM card into your phone’s SIM tray.
  2. Label Your SIM Profiles: Navigate to your phone settings (Settings > Connections / Mobile Network / Cellular). Rename your Jio eSIM to “Home” and the physical SIM to “Travel”.
  3. Set Default Data Line: Set the Mobile Data preference to the local physical SIM to ensure all app usage and browsing use local data.
  4. Set Default Voice Line: Keep your Jio eSIM as the primary line for calls/SMS if you need to receive OTPs or urgent calls.
  5. Disable Data Roaming on Home eSIM: Go into your Jio eSIM profile settings and toggle Data Roaming OFF. This prevents hidden background data charges from your home network while ensuring SMS functions normally.

3. Managing a 3-SIM Scenario: Airtel (Physical) + Jio (eSIM) + International SIM#

What happens if you already use two numbers at home—say, Airtel as a Physical SIM and Jio as an eSIM—and you want to add a third SIM for international travel?

Understanding Smartphone Hardware Limitations#

Smartphones can store multiple eSIM profiles in software memory, but standard dual-SIM smartphones (including single-physical-slot models like iPhones, flagship Samsung devices, and dual-slot phones like modern Realme or Vivo series) can only have two active physical/cellular connections simultaneously.


Stored Lines in Phone:
├── Airtel (Physical SIM)
├── Jio (eSIM Profile)
└── International (Physical SIM)

Active Limit: Max 2 at any given time!

Why You Can’t Just “Software Toggle” Your Way Around a Single Physical Slot#

If your phone has only one physical SIM slot, you physically cannot keep both the Airtel physical card and the International physical card inside the phone at the same time.

To use the International SIM in a single-slot device, you must physically remove the Airtel SIM card.

Recommended Travel Configurations#

Option A: Keep Jio (eSIM) Active for OTPs + Use Local International (Physical) SIM for Data#

  • Physical Slot: Replace the Airtel SIM with the International Physical SIM.
  • eSIM Settings: Keep Jio eSIM toggled ON.
  • Data Selection: Select International SIM for mobile data.
  • Result: You receive bank OTPs on Jio, get cheap internet on the local SIM, and the Airtel SIM stays safely stored in your wallet.

Option B: Keep Airtel (Physical) Active for OTPs + Turn Off Jio eSIM#

  • Physical Slot: Replace the Airtel SIM? No, keep Airtel Physical SIM in place.
  • eSIM Settings: Toggle Jio eSIM OFF in settings.
  • International Line: Use an International eSIM profile (purchased from providers like Airalo or Holafly) downloaded onto your phone.
  • Result: You use Airtel physical + International eSIM simultaneously.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)#

What happens to an eSIM when I turn it off in Settings?#

Nothing disappears. Toggling off an eSIM is like switching off a light switch—the digital SIM profile remains securely stored on your device’s eUICC chip. When you return to India, simply turn the toggle back ON to instantly reconnect to the network.

Will I receive banking OTPs abroad on my Jio/Airtel eSIM?#

Yes. As long as the eSIM profile is toggled ON and your Indian carrier has international roaming enabled for basic SMS (or standard incoming SMS connectivity), you will receive text messages and OTPs seamlessly.

Does turning off a SIM delete my phone number or plan?#

No. Deactivating or turning off a SIM profile in software—or taking out a physical SIM card—does not cancel your service or delete your eSIM. Your telecom provider account remains unchanged.
