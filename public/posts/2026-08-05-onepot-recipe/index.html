Easy One-Pot Cooking: From Cupcakes to Pulao, Biryani & Palak Recipes#

Cooking doesn’t have to be complicated. With a few pantry staples, fresh vegetables, and a pressure cooker, you can prepare delicious meals with minimal effort.

This guide combines easy baking, rice dishes, one-pot meals, and spinach recipes into one practical collection.

Easy Fruit Cupcakes#

Ingredients#

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup curd
  • ½ cup soyabean oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Choose one:

  • ½ cup mashed banana
  • ½ cup chopped apple
  • ½ cup blueberries

Method#

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, salt and sugar.
  3. Beat eggs with curd, oil and vanilla.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  5. Fold in your chosen fruit.
  6. Fill cupcake liners three-quarters full.
  7. Bake for 18–22 minutes.
  8. Cool before serving.

Variations#

  • Banana Cupcakes
  • Apple Cinnamon Cupcakes
  • Blueberry Cupcakes
  • Mixed Fruit Cupcakes

Chicken Pulao#

Ingredients#

Rice#

  • 1½ cups basmati rice

Chicken#

  • 500 g chicken

Vegetables#

  • 1 carrot
  • 8 beans
  • 1 potato

Aromatics#

  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Green chilies

Whole Spices#

  • Bay leaf
  • Cinnamon
  • Cardamom
  • Cloves
  • Cumin

Powder Spices#

  • Turmeric
  • Coriander powder
  • Garam masala
  • Salt

Cooking#

  1. Fry whole spices.
  2. Brown onions.
  3. Add ginger-garlic.
  4. Cook chicken.
  5. Add vegetables.
  6. Add tomato and curd.
  7. Add soaked rice.
  8. Pour water.
  9. Pressure cook for one whistle.
  10. Rest for 10 minutes.

Serve with:

  • Raita
  • Salad
  • Pickle

Beef Pulao#

Ingredients#

  • 500 g beef
  • 1½ cups basmati rice
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Curd
  • Whole spices
  • Garam masala

Method#

  1. Pressure cook beef until tender.
  2. Reserve stock.
  3. Prepare onion masala.
  4. Add beef.
  5. Mix rice.
  6. Pour reserved stock.
  7. Pressure cook one whistle.
  8. Rest before serving.

Palak Pulao#

Ingredients#

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups spinach
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Ginger-garlic
  • Green chili
  • Cumin
  • Garam masala
  • Salt

Optional

  • Peas
  • Carrot
  • Beans
  • Paneer
  • Cashews

Method#

  1. Blend blanched spinach.
  2. Cook onion masala.
  3. Add spinach puree.
  4. Add soaked rice.
  5. Pour water.
  6. Pressure cook one whistle.
  7. Rest 10 minutes.

Serve with

  • Plain curd
  • Boondi raita
  • Pickle
  • Papad

Pressure Cooker Tips#

Rice-to-Water Ratio#

RiceWater
1 cup Basmati1½ cups (pressure cooker)
1 cup Basmati1¾ cups (pot)

Resting Time#

Always allow natural pressure release for 10 minutes.

This keeps rice fluffy and prevents grains from breaking.

Flavor Boosters#

  • Ghee

Meal Planning Tips#

  • Wash and chop vegetables on weekends.
  • Marinate chicken overnight.
  • Keep ginger-garlic paste ready.
  • Soak rice for 20–30 minutes before cooking.
  • Store fried onions in an airtight container.
  • Freeze homemade stock for richer pulao and biryani.

Final Thoughts#

Whether you’re baking fruit cupcakes, preparing a comforting vegetable khichdi, making a quick chicken pulao, cooking a festive beef biryani, or enjoying a nutritious palak pulao, these recipes focus on simplicity, everyday ingredients, and maximum flavor with minimal cleanup.

A pressure cooker and a handful of pantry staples are enough to create an entire week’s worth of satisfying homemade meals.
