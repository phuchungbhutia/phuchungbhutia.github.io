### Part 1: Meta-Prompt Engineering for Multimodal Research

Designing an effective prompt requires clear structure: defining roles, specifying context, and setting strict output formats. To test this framework, we constructed a comprehensive research prompt on physical and contemplative mastery—focusing on walking, breathing, and meditation—complete with requests for visual aids, infographics, audio concepts, and step-by-step guides.

```text
Prompt: Research expert techniques for walking, breathing, and meditation. 
For each practice, provide:
1. Detailed Step-by-Step Guides (movements, postures, mental focus).
2. Visual Aids (Static images, Infographics, Animated GIF concepts, Short Video & Audio concepts).
3. Emphasis on Accuracy (alignment with physiological and contemplative standards).
4. Expert Perspective (nuances, advanced cues, and framing).

```

### Part 2: The Nine Purification Breaths (རླུང་རོ་དགུ་ཕྲུགས)

In Tibetan subtle-body traditions, the Nine Purification Breaths clear the energetic channels (*nadis*) of stale wind (*prana*) and mental obscurations before formal meditation or daily activities.

```
       [Left Channel]         [Central Channel]        [Right Channel]
        (Blue / Water)          (Gold / Wisdom)         (Red / Fire)
       Clears Aversion         Clears Ignorance        Clears Attachment

```

#### Phase 1: The Right Channel (Physical & Attachment)

1. **Inhale:** Block the right nostril with your ring finger. Inhale deeply through the left nostril, visualizing pure white light entering your frame.
2. **Hold:** Retain the breath briefly. Allow the light to scan the body for physical tension.
3. **Exhale:** Release smoothly through the right nostril. Visualize dark smoke leaving, carrying away physical pain, fatigue, and attachment.

#### Phase 2: The Left Channel (Mental & Aversion)

4. **Inhale:** Block the left nostril. Inhale deeply through the right nostril, visualizing cool blue light.
5. **Hold:** Retain the breath. Experience your mind becoming expansive and still.
6. **Exhale:** Release through the left nostril. Visualize grey mist escaping, expelling anger, frustration, and mental noise.

#### Phase 3: The Central Channel (Spiritual & Ignorance)

7. **Inhale:** Inhale through both nostrils simultaneously. Visualize luminous golden light filling the central column along your spine.
8. **Hold:** Rest your attention at the navel center. Maintain non-dual presence.
9. **Exhale:** Exhale through both nostrils, releasing dark shadows to reveal your inherent Buddha nature.

### Part 3: Dawn Integration — Khenpo Ngakchung’s Morning Supplication

Combining breathwork with sacred recitation immediately upon waking aligns your "Wind-Mind" (*Prana-Chitta*) for the day ahead.

> **Tibetan:** ཐོ་རེངས་མལ་ནས་ལྡང་བའི་དུས། དུས་གསུམ་སངས་རྒྱས་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀྱི་ངོ་བོ་དཔལ་ལྡན་རྩ་བའི་བླ་མ་རིན་པོ་ཆེ་མཁྱེན་ནོ། །
> **Phonetic/Translation:** "When rising from bed in the early morning, say: Essence of all buddhas of the three times, glorious, precious root guru, think of me!"

#### Morning Sequence & Transmutations

| Phase | Physical & Energetic Focus | Supplication Line | Inner Transmutation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Body** | Sit in 7-point posture; perform Right Channel breath. | *"Bless me so that bliss arises in my body."* | Transmutes lethargy into physical vitality. |
| **Speech** | Focus on throat lotus; perform Left Channel breath. | *"Bless me so that my speech blazes with power."* | Transmutes idle chatter into truthful efficacy. |
| **Mind** | Rest in heart center; perform Central Channel breath. | *"Bless me so that realization dawns in my mind."* | Dissolves self-grasping into spacious awareness. |
| **Life** | Reflect on impermanence. | *"Bless me so that I can give up thoughts of this life."* | Shifts orientation from temporary desires to awakening. |
| **Heart** | Generate Bodhicitta. | *"Bless me that the supreme heart of awakening arises."* | Expands individual awareness into universal compassion. |

By keeping this supplication beneath your pillow and reciting it alongside the Nine Purification Breaths at dawn, you bridge conscious waking awareness directly into spiritual practice.
