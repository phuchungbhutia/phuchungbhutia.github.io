# 8 Reusable Master Prompts for Professional Image Design in ChatGPT

AI image generation becomes much more useful when you stop treating every image as a one-off request and start building reusable prompt templates. The eight master prompts below cover common design tasks such as social media advertising, product photography, event flyers, YouTube thumbnails, book covers, restaurant menus, educational infographics, and mobile app mockups.

Each prompt uses replaceable fields such as [BRAND], [HEADLINE], [COLORS], and [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Replace those fields with your own information, then paste the complete prompt into ChatGPT Images. OpenAI recommends clear, descriptive prompts that identify the purpose, subject, setting, visual style, and important constraints. It also recommends making small, targeted revisions instead of rewriting everything after every generation.

## What Are Master Prompts?

A master prompt is a reusable instruction template designed for a particular type of visual output. Instead of writing a vague request like "Make me a nice restaurant menu," you define the complete design requirements once, including restaurant information, menu sections, typography, colours, layout, imagery, accuracy requirements, print format, and quality controls.

The next time you need a menu, you simply replace the variables. This creates a small personal image-prompt library that can be reused for different projects.

## The Eight Master Prompts

### 1. Branded Social Post Generator

Creates polished promotional graphics for products, services, announcements, launches, sales, and brand campaigns.

> Create a premium, attention-grabbing social media advertisement for [BRAND/PRODUCT].
> 
> Campaign purpose: [PRODUCT LAUNCH / SALE / AWARENESS / ANNOUNCEMENT / PROMOTION]
> Main headline: "[HEADLINE]"
> Supporting text: "[SUBHEADLINE]"
> Call to action: "[CTA]"
> Brand: [BRAND NAME]
> Product/service: [PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]
> Target audience: [TARGET AUDIENCE]
> Visual style: [MODERN / PREMIUM / MINIMAL / BOLD / LUXURY / SPORTY / PLAYFUL]
> Brand colors: [COLORS]
> 
> Create a polished commercial composition with strong visual hierarchy. Make the product or subject the primary focal point. Use professional advertising photography, realistic lighting, natural shadows, subtle depth of field, clean composition, and carefully controlled negative space.
> 
> Typography should be bold, highly readable, correctly spelled, and professionally arranged. Keep the headline prominent without covering the main subject. Include the logo only if provided. Do not invent logos, claims, statistics, awards, certifications, prices, or product features.
> 
> Make the final image look like a professionally designed campaign from a major advertising agency.
> Format: [1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9 / 9:16]
> Resolution: high-resolution, sharp, print-quality detail.

**How to use:** Replace the bracketed fields. For example, change [BRAND NAME] to Mountain Brew, [HEADLINE] to Taste the Mountains, [CTA] to Order Now, and [COLORS] to deep green, cream, and gold. This prompt is particularly useful for Instagram posts, Facebook advertisements, promotional banners, and campaign graphics.

### 2. Hero Product Ad Shot

Creates a high-end commercial product photograph suitable for advertising, ecommerce, campaigns, and promotional material.

> Create a photorealistic premium product advertisement for [PRODUCT].
> 
> Product: [PRODUCT NAME + DETAILED DESCRIPTION]
> Brand: [BRAND]
> Campaign message: "[HEADLINE]"
> Supporting text: "[OPTIONAL SUBTEXT]"
> CTA: "[CTA]"
> Environment: [STUDIO / LUXURY INTERIOR / NATURE / DESKTOP / PREMIUM LIFESTYLE SETTING]
> Background: [BACKGROUND DESCRIPTION]
> Color palette: [COLORS]
> 
> Create a cinematic hero product photograph. Place the product as the dominant focal point with accurate proportions, realistic materials, authentic textures, and precise product details.
> 
> Use professional commercial lighting with a strong key light, soft fill, realistic reflections, controlled highlights, natural shadows, subtle atmospheric depth, and premium photographic contrast. Use an elegant composition with enough negative space for advertising copy. The product must remain completely recognizable and must not be distorted.
> 
> Typography, if required, should be minimal, clean, correctly spelled, and integrated naturally into the composition. Do not invent product features, buttons, specifications, logos, packaging text, certifications, or claims.
> 
> Style: high-end commercial product photography, luxury advertising, photorealistic, editorial quality.
> Format: [1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9 / 9:16]
> Output should look like a finished professional advertisement.

**How to use:** Give the model as much useful product information as possible. For example, specify "Stainless-steel insulated travel bottle, matte black finish, 750 ml capacity, cylindrical shape, screw cap." If you have an actual product photograph, upload it and tell ChatGPT what should remain unchanged.

### 3. Launch Event Flyer

Creates professional posters and flyers for conferences, workshops, webinars, launches, festivals, and community events.

> Design a professional event flyer for [EVENT NAME].
> 
> Event type: [CONFERENCE / WORKSHOP / WEBINAR / PRODUCT LAUNCH / FESTIVAL / COMMUNITY EVENT]
> Main headline: "[EVENT NAME]"
> Theme: "[EVENT THEME]"
> Date: "[DATE]"
> Time: "[TIME]"
> Venue: "[VENUE]"
> Location: "[LOCATION]"
> Speaker/guest: "[NAME + TITLE]"
> CTA: "[REGISTER NOW / BOOK YOUR SEAT / JOIN US]"
> Website/contact: "[WEBSITE / PHONE / EMAIL]"
> Organizer: "[ORGANIZATION]"
> 
> Create a polished, modern event-poster composition with a strong visual hierarchy.
> Primary visual: [DESCRIBE MAIN IMAGE OR SUBJECT]
> Use [COLOR PALETTE] with a professional combination of typography, photography, shapes, gradients, icons, and controlled visual effects.
> 
> Make the event name immediately readable. Organize date, time, venue, speakers, and CTA into clear information blocks. Use realistic photography or sophisticated editorial imagery appropriate to the event. Maintain generous spacing and avoid visual clutter.
> 
> All supplied text must be reproduced exactly, with correct spelling and punctuation. Do not invent additional information. Do not add fake sponsors, logos, speakers, locations, statistics, awards, or claims.
> 
> Style: [CORPORATE / PREMIUM / TECHNOLOGY / CREATIVE / ELEGANT / FESTIVE]
> Format: [A4 PORTRAIT / 4:5 / 16:9 / 9:16]
> Create a finished, publication-ready flyer.

**How to use:** This works particularly well when the event has a lot of information. Keep the hierarchy simple: Event, Theme, Date/Time, Venue, Speaker, CTA. For dense posters, explicitly asking for readable text and clear information blocks helps the model understand which information matters most.

### 4. Click-Worthy Video Thumbnail

Creates attention-focused YouTube and video thumbnails while keeping the visual connected to the actual video topic.

> Create a highly engaging YouTube/video thumbnail for:
> 
> Video title: "[VIDEO TITLE]"
> Topic: "[VIDEO TOPIC]"
> Target audience: "[AUDIENCE]"
> Main message: "[KEY MESSAGE]"
> Thumbnail text: "[3 TO 7 WORDS MAXIMUM]"
> Main subject: [PERSON / PRODUCT / OBJECT / SCREENSHOT / CONCEPT]
> 
> Create a dramatic, instantly understandable thumbnail designed for strong visual attention at small screen sizes. Use one dominant focal subject with an expressive but natural pose. If a person is included, preserve realistic anatomy, facial proportions, skin texture, hair, clothing, and natural expression.
> 
> Use strong foreground/background separation, dramatic lighting, high contrast, controlled shadows, bold typography, and a simple visual story. Keep the thumbnail uncluttered. Use large readable text with a maximum of a few words. Make the most important visual element occupy the majority of the frame.
> 
> Use visual cues such as arrows, circles, highlights, glow, screenshots, or contrast only when they genuinely support the topic. Do not create misleading imagery that contradicts the video topic. Do not add fake statistics, fake screenshots, fake quotes, fake logos, or unsupported claims.
> 
> Style: [TECH / EDUCATION / FINANCE / GAMING / AI / NEWS / REVIEW / ENTERTAINMENT]
> Format: 16:9
> Composition must remain readable when displayed as a small thumbnail.

**How to use:** The most important field is the thumbnail text. Do not try to put the entire video title on the thumbnail. Instead, if the title is "How I Built a Website Using AI in One Hour," use thumbnail text like "BUILT WITH AI." The image should communicate the idea even before the viewer reads the full title.

### 5. Bestseller Book Cover Design

Creates an original, professional book-cover concept for ebooks, print books, guides, novels, and educational publications.

> Design a professional bestselling-style book cover for:
> 
> Book title: "[BOOK TITLE]"
> Subtitle: "[SUBTITLE]"
> Author: "[AUTHOR NAME]"
> Genre: "[GENRE]"
> Core subject: "[MAIN IDEA / STORY / TOPIC]"
> Target reader: "[TARGET AUDIENCE]"
> Desired mood: [MINIMAL / PREMIUM / MYSTERIOUS / INSPIRING / ACADEMIC / DRAMATIC / MODERN]
> 
> Create a sophisticated publishing-quality book cover with a strong visual concept. The title must be the dominant typographic element. Subtitle and author name should have clear secondary hierarchy.
> 
> Create an original visual composition that communicates the book's subject without copying the design, artwork, typography, or trade dress of any existing book.
> Use: [COLOR PALETTE]
> Typography style: [MODERN SANS / CLASSIC SERIF / EDITORIAL / BOLD DISPLAY]
> Include: [SYMBOL / OBJECT / LANDSCAPE / ABSTRACT FORM / CHARACTER / PHOTOGRAPHIC SUBJECT]
> 
> Use balanced spacing, professional typography, subtle texture, controlled contrast, and premium editorial design. Do not add fake reviews, awards, bestseller badges, sales figures, publisher logos, quotes, or endorsements unless explicitly supplied. Ensure every supplied word is spelled correctly.
> 
> Create a clean front-cover design suitable for ebook and print publication.
> Format: [2:3 / 6x9 INCH / A4 / CUSTOM]
> High-resolution, print-ready appearance.

**How to use:** Describe the idea behind the book, not only the title. For example, define the core subject as "Mindfulness for busy working parents" and the desired mood as "Calm, premium, modern, contemplative." The model can then build a visual concept around the subject. Keep the design original rather than asking it to copy the exact appearance of an existing book.

### 6. Restaurant Menu Card

Creates clean, readable menus for restaurants, cafes, hotels, bakeries, and food businesses.

> Design a premium restaurant menu for:
> 
> Restaurant: "[RESTAURANT NAME]"
> Cuisine: "[CUISINE TYPE]"
> Theme: [MODERN / TRADITIONAL / LUXURY / RUSTIC / MINIMAL / CAFE]
> Menu sections: [COFFEE / STARTERS / MAIN COURSE / DESSERTS / BEVERAGES]
> Menu items and prices: [PASTE EXACT MENU HERE]
> Address: "[ADDRESS]"
> Contact: "[PHONE / WEBSITE]"
> 
> Create an elegant, highly readable restaurant menu with a carefully structured information hierarchy. Use a visual style inspired by: [STYLE DESCRIPTION]
> Color palette: [COLORS]
> 
> Include tasteful food photography or illustrations only where appropriate. Use subtle decorative elements, botanical details, textures, borders, icons, or patterns without making the menu visually crowded. Arrange categories, item names, descriptions, and prices consistently.
> 
> All supplied menu names and prices must be reproduced exactly. Do not change spelling, currency, quantities, ingredients, or prices. Do not invent dishes, prices, ingredients, nutritional claims, certifications, awards, or restaurant information.
> 
> Make the menu practical for real-world printing and customer use. Typography must be exceptionally readable.
> Format: [A4 PORTRAIT / A5 / 4:5 / CUSTOM]
> Create a polished professional restaurant menu suitable for print.

**How to use:** Paste the actual menu under the menu items and prices field. The instruction to preserve supplied prices is critical. AI should not be allowed to complete missing prices or invent menu items. For a real printed menu, you should still proofread the final image before publication.

### 7. Explainer Infographic

Turns complicated information into a structured visual explanation using sections, icons, diagrams, numbers, and a clear reading flow.

> Create a professional educational infographic explaining:
> 
> Topic: "[TOPIC]"
> Target audience: "[AUDIENCE]"
> Main title: "[TITLE]"
> Introduction/message: "[ONE-SENTENCE EXPLANATION]"
> Information to visualize:
> 1. [POINT 1]
> 2. [POINT 2]
> 3. [POINT 3]
> 4. [POINT 4]
> 5. [POINT 5]
> Key statistics: [STATISTICS, IF ANY]
> Call to action: "[CTA, IF REQUIRED]"
> 
> Create a clean vertical infographic with a logical top-to-bottom reading flow.
> Visual hierarchy:
> 1. Main title
> 2. Short explanation
> 3. Numbered sections
> 4. Supporting visuals
> 5. Key facts
> 6. Final takeaway
> 
> Use simple icons, diagrams, illustrations, charts, arrows, numbered steps, and visual grouping where appropriate.
> Color palette: [COLORS]
> Design style: [MODERN / CORPORATE / MEDICAL / EDUCATIONAL / MINIMAL / FRIENDLY]
> 
> Use large, readable typography and generous spacing. Every supplied fact, number, percentage, date, label, and sentence must remain accurate and unchanged. Do not invent statistics, sources, medical claims, scientific findings, quotations, or references. Avoid unnecessary decorative elements that interfere with comprehension.
> 
> The infographic should communicate the subject clearly even when viewed quickly.
> Format: vertical [A4 / 4:5 / 9:16]
> High-resolution, publication-ready design.

**How to use:** This prompt is especially useful for government information, educational material, financial explainers, public awareness campaigns, scientific concepts, process diagrams, workplace instructions, and social-media educational posts. For information-heavy designs, explicitly request sharp text rendering.

### 8. App Screen Mockup

Creates realistic mobile application interfaces for product concepts, portfolios, pitch decks, prototypes, and UI presentations.

> Design a polished modern mobile app interface for:
> 
> App name: "[APP NAME]"
> App purpose: "[WHAT THE APP DOES]"
> Target users: "[TARGET USERS]"
> Primary screen: [HOME / SEARCH / DASHBOARD / BOOKING / PROFILE / OTHER]
> Required features:
> 1. [FEATURE]
> 2. [FEATURE]
> 3. [FEATURE]
> 4. [FEATURE]
> 5. [FEATURE]
> Primary CTA: "[BUTTON TEXT]"
> Brand colors: [COLORS]
> Design language: [MINIMAL / PREMIUM / MATERIAL / IOS-LIKE / MODERN SAAS / FINTECH / TRAVEL]
> 
> Create a realistic smartphone UI mockup showing a professionally designed application screen. Use a clear information hierarchy, consistent spacing, realistic navigation elements, intuitive buttons, cards, icons, search fields, tabs, and content sections.
> 
> Make the interface feel like a real production application rather than a generic concept. Show realistic mobile dimensions, consistent typography, aligned components, appropriate iconography, accessible contrast, realistic spacing, clear CTA, coherent navigation, and polished interaction states where appropriate.
> 
> If multiple screens are requested, maintain exactly the same design system, colors, typography, icons, spacing, and component styles across all screens. Do not copy an existing company's interface or trademarked design. Do not invent unsupported features or data. All supplied interface text must be spelled correctly.
> 
> Presentation: Place the UI inside a realistic premium smartphone mockup with subtle reflections and studio lighting, unless a flat UI screenshot is requested.
> Format: [9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1]
> High-resolution product-design presentation suitable for a portfolio, pitch deck, prototype presentation, or app launch.

**How to use:** Start with one screen. Once the visual language works, ask for additional screens while explicitly telling ChatGPT to keep the same design system. Consistency between screens matters more than making every individual screen visually impressive.

## Universal Quality-Control Add-On

This section can be attached to almost any of the eight prompts to ensure high-quality output.

> FINAL QUALITY CONTROL:
> Before generating the image, internally check the composition for:
> - correct spelling
> - accurate supplied text
> - realistic proportions
> - consistent typography
> - clean alignment
> - readable hierarchy
> - realistic lighting
> - natural shadows
> - coherent perspective
> - no distorted objects
> - no duplicated elements
> - no accidental extra people
> - no unwanted logos
> - no invented claims or information
> - no unnecessary visual clutter
> 
> Prioritize clarity, accuracy, professional composition, and visual realism over excessive decoration.

This works as a useful final layer because it reminds the model which constraints matter most.

## How to Use the Prompt Library

The easiest workflow is to treat the prompts like design templates rather than prompts that must be rewritten every time.

1. Choose the design type. Use the Social Post Generator for Instagram or Facebook advertisements, the Hero Product Ad for product advertising images, the Launch Event Flyer for conference or workshop posters, the Video Thumbnail for YouTube, the Book Cover for ebooks or printed books, the Menu Card for cafes or restaurants, the Explainer Infographic for educational information, and the App Screen Mockup for app concepts.
2. Copy the complete prompt. Do not copy only the first few lines. The value of the master prompt comes from the combination of purpose, content, composition, visual style, constraints, and quality control.
3. Replace the variables. Anything inside square brackets is a variable. For example, change [BRAND] to Mountain Brew Coffee and [COLORS] to forest green, cream, and warm gold.
4. Add your exact information. For text-heavy designs, paste the exact information you want reproduced. This is especially important for prices, dates, names, addresses, phone numbers, statistics, legal information, government terminology, and product specifications. Do not ask the model to guess missing information.
5. Generate the first version. ChatGPT Images supports both new image creation and editing of existing images. You can also specify aspect ratios directly or use the interface controls.
6. Refine instead of starting again. If the first result is close, give a targeted instruction. For example, "Keep the composition exactly the same. Make the headline 20 percent smaller and increase the empty space around the product," or "Keep all supplied text unchanged. Simplify the background and improve readability." OpenAI recommends small, targeted revisions because they make it easier to maintain the successful parts of an image while correcting specific problems.

## The Most Useful Prompt Formula

You do not actually need a huge prompt for every image. A strong reusable structure is:

- CREATE
- SUBJECT
- PURPOSE
- CONTENT
- COMPOSITION
- STYLE
- FORMAT
- CONSTRAINTS
- QUALITY CONTROL

For example:

> Create an educational infographic about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
> Show: [KEY INFORMATION]
> Use: [STYLE], [COLORS], [LAYOUT]
> Format: [A4 PORTRAIT]
> Keep all supplied facts and numbers unchanged. Do not invent information. Use clear typography and strong visual hierarchy.

This reflects a broader principle in image prompting: specificity matters more than decorative prompt language. OpenAI guidance recommends describing the purpose, main subject, setting, visual style, and important constraints clearly rather than relying on vague descriptions.

## One Library, Many Uses

The real advantage of these prompts is that they are not tied to one industry. The same eight templates can be adapted for business, education, government, tourism, restaurants, YouTube, publishing, ecommerce, social media, software, events, and public awareness campaigns. 

For example, the Explainer Infographic can become a government awareness poster, while the App Screen Mockup can become a fintech dashboard or travel application. The prompts therefore work more like a small visual design system than a collection of individual image prompts.

Save these eight templates as your Reusable Image Design Library. Instead of asking ChatGPT to "Make me a professional poster," start with the appropriate master prompt, replace the variables, provide the exact content, generate the first version, and then refine it with small targeted changes. That gives you a repeatable workflow for turning the same basic idea into a social advertisement, product campaign, flyer, thumbnail, book cover, menu, infographic, or app concept.
