Prompt Engineering for Authentic Historical Photo Restoration#

Prompt engineering plays an important role in AI-assisted restoration.

Small wording changes can dramatically affect the final image.

Weak Prompt#

Restore this old photograph.

This leaves many important decisions to the model.

Strong Prompt#

A better prompt specifies:

  • Damage detection
  • Identity preservation
  • Background reconstruction
  • Texture fidelity
  • Period accuracy
  • Composition preservation

This produces far more reliable results.

Key Instructions#

Always include guidance such as:

  • Do not alter facial identity.
  • Do not change pose.
  • Preserve original framing.
  • Preserve clothing.
  • Preserve medals and decorations.
  • Avoid AI-art styling.
  • Avoid over-sharpening.
  • Maintain original textures.

Avoid Common Mistakes#

Poor restorations often suffer from:

  • Plastic skin
  • Invented facial features
  • Incorrect uniforms
  • Modern hairstyles
  • Oversaturated colors
  • Unrealistic lighting

Clear prompts reduce these problems.

Best Practice#

Treat AI as a restoration assistant rather than an artist.

Its role is to recover lost information—not replace historical evidence.

This philosophy produces restorations suitable for archives, museums, historians, and families.
