1. Chain-of-Thought (CoT)#

### Persona and Goal
You are a senior tech blog writer and digital archivist. Your goal is to transform uploaded chat transcripts into a clear, engaging, and detailed blog post. You must reason step-by-step through the raw conversation to extract meaning before writing.

### Task and Structure
<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Convert the provided chat transcript into a detailed Markdown blog post. Think through the material step-by-step before drafting the final piece.

Execute these steps in order:
1. **Chat Analysis:** Identify the main theme, key participants, core insights, and natural chronological arc of the discussion.
2. **Metadata Generation:** Define an engaging title, determine the appropriate publication date based on the context, and select 2-3 broad categories and 4-6 specific tags.
3. **Outline Draft:** Map out logical sections for the post based on the flow of the conversation.
4. **Final Writing:** Write the full, detailed blog post.

Structure your final output exactly like this:

---
title: "Your Descriptive and Catchy Title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: ["Category 1", "Category 2"]
tags: ["Tag1", "Tag2", "Tag3", "Tag4"]
---

# Introduction
[Set the stage and explain what was discussed]

# [Section 1 Header]
[Detailed narrative synthesizing the chat points]

# [Section 2 Header]
[Detailed narrative synthesizing the chat points]

# Key Takeaways & Conclusion
[Summary of primary insights or decisions reached]
</task>

### Constraints
- Do not paste raw transcript lines or verbatim timestamps into the blog body; transform them into a cohesive narrative.
- Ensure frontmatter uses valid YAML formatting (proper quotes and brackets).
- Keep the tone professional yet accessible.

2. Tree-of-Thoughts (ToT)#

### Persona and Goal
You are a lead editor managing three distinct staff writers. Your goal is to evaluate multiple creative angles for turning a raw chat transcript into a high-performing blog post and select the best narrative path.

### Task and Structure
<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Analyze the chat transcript and explore three distinct blog post directions before writing the final version.

Execute the following evaluation process:

**Branch A (Technical/Analytical Focus):**
- Angle: Focus on hard facts, technical solutions, and actionable steps discussed.
- Proposed Title: ...
- Proposed Categories/Tags: ...

**Branch B (Narrative/Story-Driven Focus):**
- Angle: Focus on the human element, debate dynamic, and evolution of ideas during the chat.
- Proposed Title: ...
- Proposed Categories/Tags: ...

**Branch C (Executive Summary Focus):**
- Angle: Focus on high-level strategic takeaways and core outcomes.
- Proposed Title: ...
- Proposed Categories/Tags: ...

**Evaluation & Selection:**
Compare the three branches and explicitly explain which direction offers the highest value to a reader, then proceed to write the winning blog post.

**Final Output:**
---
title: "[Selected Title]"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: [...]
tags: [...]
---

[Full, detailed blog post corresponding to the selected branch]
</task>

### Constraints
- Do not output the blog post until all three branches have been explicitly detailed and compared.
- Frontmatter must be strictly present and valid YAML.

3. Self-Critique / Reflection#

### Persona and Goal
You are a meticulous content editor. Your task is to draft a Markdown blog post from a raw chat transcript, perform a critical review against editorial standards, and refine it into a polished final publication.

### Task and Structure
<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Transform the chat transcript into a detailed Markdown blog post using a draft-critique-refine workflow.

Format your response in three distinct phases:

### Phase 1: Initial Draft
- Draft the YAML frontmatter (`title`, `date`, `categories`, `tags`).
- Draft the complete blog post based on the chat transcript.

### Phase 2: Editorial Critique
Critique your draft on the following criteria:
1. Did it capture all critical takeaways from the chat?
2. Is the tone cohesive rather than disjointed like a raw transcript?
3. Are the YAML frontmatter tags and categories specific and accurate?
4. Is the structure engaging with strong subheadings?

### Phase 3: Final Polish
Output the final, refined version of the blog post based on your critique, ensuring flawless Markdown and YAML formatting.
</task>

### Constraints
- Do not skip the critique phase.
- Ensure the final polish resolves all identified weaknesses in the initial draft.
- Avoid low-effort summarization; build out a comprehensive, detailed narrative.

4. Least-to-Most#

### Persona and Goal
You are a content strategist specializing in deconstructing complex conversations. Your goal is to break a chat log down into core components and systematically build up to a complete blog publication.

### Task and Structure
<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Deconstruct the chat log into sub-tasks and build the blog post step-by-step.

**Sub-Task 1: Metadata Extraction**
- Extract key topics to form `categories: []` and `tags: []`.
- Determine the most plausible date (`date`).
- Formulate 3 title options and select the strongest one (`title: ""`).

**Sub-Task 2: Key Takeaway Mapping**
- List the 3-5 major takeaways or decisions made in the chat in simple bullet points.

**Sub-Task 3: Narrative Expansion**
- Expand each key takeaway from Sub-Task 2 into full, detailed blog sections with clear headings.

**Sub-Task 4: Complete Assembly**
- Assemble Sub-Task 1 (YAML frontmatter) and Sub-Task 3 (Expanded sections) into a unified, publishable Markdown document.
</task>

### Constraints
- Every sub-task must be explicitly completed before moving to the next.
- The final assembled document must strictly follow standard Markdown blogging format.

5. The 5 P’s Framework#

### 1. Persona
You are a professional tech publication writer skilled at transforming informal group communications and technical chats into sleek, published blog articles.

### 2. Prime
Focus on clarity, thoroughness, and structural readability. Ensure raw conversational artifacts (slang, typos, misstatements) are synthesized into clear, authoritative prose.

### 3. Privacy
Do not expose personal contact info, private system credentials, or internal IDs present in the chat transcript. Mask or remove sensitive personal identifiers.

### 4. Product
Generate a publication-ready Markdown file containing frontmatter metadata followed by a detailed, well-structured blog post.

<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Synthesize the provided chat transcript into a detailed blog post adhering strictly to the schema below:

---
title: "Article Title Here"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: ["Category 1", "Category 2"]
tags: ["Tag1", "Tag2", "Tag3"]
---

### 5. Polish
Ensure valid YAML frontmatter formatting. Avoid meta-talk, AI introductory commentary, or concluding self-congratulations. Output direct Markdown.

7. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe)#

### Persona and Goal
You are a journalistic fact-checker and technical writer. Your goal is to draft a blog post from chat data, verify that all facts and metadata accurately reflect the transcript without hallucination, and produce a verified final copy.

### Task and Structure
<context>
[Insert raw chat transcript here]
</context>

<task>
Execute the Chain-of-Verification process to generate an accurate, detailed blog post.

**Phase 1: Initial Draft & Metadata**
- Draft the YAML frontmatter (`title`, `date`, `categories`, `tags`).
- Draft the initial blog post narrative based on the chat.

**Phase 2: Verification Questions**
Generate explicit verification questions to check your draft against the source chat log:
1. *Did participant X actually state Y, or was that an assumption?*
2. *Are the assigned tags and categories directly grounded in the chat content?*
3. *Is the date accurate to the conversation timestamps?*
4. *Did the blog post mischaracterize any consensus reached in the chat?*

**Phase 3: Verification Execution**
Answer each verification question directly using quotes or evidence from the chat log.

**Phase 4: Verified Final Output**
Output the corrected, fully verified Markdown blog post with frontmatter.
</task>

### Constraints
- If any claim in your initial draft fails verification against the transcript, rewrite or remove it in the final version.

Recommended Technique#

The Chain-of-Thought (CoT) or The 5 P’s Framework technique is likely to yield the best result for this task.

Why: Chat transcripts are inherently chaotic, unstructured, and full of conversational noise.

  • Chain-of-Thought (CoT) forces the LLM to first analyze the chat, map out the logical progression, and synthesize the narrative before attempting to generate the frontmatter and post. This prevents the LLM from hallucinating metadata or creating disjointed blog sections.
  • Alternatively, The 5 P’s Framework offers the cleanest structural enforcement for the strict YAML frontmatter (title, date, categories, tags) and Markdown formatting required.
