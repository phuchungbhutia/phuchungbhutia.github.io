The Definitive Guide to Signing PDFs on Every Device

August 4, 2026 · 3 min · Phuchung Bhutia

Handling physical paperwork is rapidly becoming a relic of the past. Whether you are finalizing a business contract, approving an invoice, or filling out administrative forms, knowing how to add a clean, secure signature to a PDF directly from your device saves time and eliminates unnecessary printing.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of how to sign a PDF document using your preferred operating system or browser without needing expensive enterprise software.

Method 1: Using Adobe Acrobat Reader (Desktop - Windows & Mac)#

Adobe Acrobat Reader remains the industry standard for viewing and managing PDF documents. Its native Fill & Sign tool provides a smooth way to type, draw, or insert a signature.

  1. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader.
  2. Select Fill & Sign from the tools menu in the right-hand panel.
  3. Click Sign Yourself from the top action bar.
  4. Click Add Signature.
  5. Choose one of the three options provided:
    • Type: Input your name and select a stylized font.
    • Draw: Use a trackpad, mouse, or stylus to write your signature.
    • Image: Upload a scanned image of your actual physical signature written on white paper.
  6. Click Apply.
  7. Click anywhere on the document to drop your signature, resize it as needed, and save the file.

Method 2: Using Apple Preview (macOS)#

If you use a Mac, you do not need to download third-party software. The built-in Preview app features powerful markup tools.

  1. Double-click your PDF to launch it in Preview.
  2. Click the Show Markup Toolbar icon (resembling a pen tip inside a circle) in the top-right menu.
  3. Select the Sign icon.
  4. Create your signature using one of two methods:
    • Trackpad: Use your finger to draw on the trackpad.
    • Camera: Sign a clean piece of white paper, hold it up to your Mac’s webcam, and let Preview automatically capture and digitize it.
  5. Click your saved signature to insert it into the document.
  6. Drag and resize the signature box to fit the designated line.

Method 3: Using Web Browsers (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome)#

If you need a quick signature without opening specialized desktop apps, modern web browsers offer built-in ink annotations.

  1. Right-click your PDF file, go to Open With, and select your browser (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome).
  2. Look for the Draw icon in the browser’s top floating toolbar.
  3. Adjust your ink color and thickness.
  4. Use your mouse, touch screen, or stylus to write your signature directly on the target line.
  5. Click the Save (floppy disk icon) or Download button to export the updated, signed PDF.

Method 4: Using Free Online Web Apps#

For users working on public computers or quick tasks on Chromebooks, online document editors provide a light, browser-based solution.

  1. Navigate to a trusted online editor like Smallpdf, iLovePDF, or Adobe Acrobat Web.
  2. Upload your PDF document.
  3. Select the eSign or Add Signature tool.
  4. Draw or upload your signature.
  5. Drag your signature into position and hit Apply/Finish.
  6. Download the final signed PDF to your local storage.

Choosing the Right Method#

  • For official legal documents: Adobe Acrobat Reader or native desktop apps offer the most accurate placement and security.
  • For Mac power users: Apple Preview is the fastest, cleanest option with zero app overhead.
  • For rapid turnarounds: Edge, Chrome, or online tools deliver instant results without installation requirements.
