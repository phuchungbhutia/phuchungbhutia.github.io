# Easy Fruit Cupcakes

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup curd
- ½ cup soyabean oil
- 1 tsp vanilla essence

Choose one:

- ½ cup mashed banana
- ½ cup chopped apple
- ½ cup blueberries

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
2. Mix flour, baking powder, salt and sugar.
3. Beat eggs with curd, oil and vanilla.
4. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
5. Fold in your chosen fruit.
6. Fill cupcake liners three-quarters full.
7. Bake for 18–22 minutes.
8. Cool before serving.

## Variations

- Banana Cupcakes
- Apple Cinnamon Cupcakes
- Blueberry Cupcakes
- Mixed Fruit Cupcakes

# Biryani vs Pulao

| Feature | Pulao | Biryani |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Cooking style | One-pot | Layered |
| Rice | Cooked with ingredients | Rice cooked separately |
| Flavor | Mild | Rich and aromatic |
| Cooking time | 20–30 min | 60–90 min |
| Difficulty | Easy | Medium |
| Best for | Daily meals | Special occasions |

## Pulao

- Simple
- Less spice
- Faster cooking
- One-pot recipe

## Biryani

- Rich masala
- Layered rice
- Dum cooking
- Deep flavors

# Chicken Pulao

## Ingredients

### Rice

- 1½ cups basmati rice

### Chicken

- 500 g chicken

### Vegetables

- 1 carrot
- 8 beans
- 1 potato

### Aromatics

- Onion
- Tomato
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Green chilies

### Whole Spices

- Bay leaf
- Cinnamon
- Cardamom
- Cloves
- Cumin

### Powder Spices

- Turmeric
- Coriander powder
- Garam masala
- Salt

## Cooking

1. Fry whole spices.
2. Brown onions.
3. Add ginger-garlic.
4. Cook chicken.
5. Add vegetables.
6. Add tomato and curd.
7. Add soaked rice.
8. Pour water.
9. Pressure cook for one whistle.
10. Rest for 10 minutes.

Serve with:

- Raita
- Salad
- Pickle

# Beef Pulao

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef
- 1½ cups basmati rice
- Onion
- Tomato
- Curd
- Whole spices
- Garam masala

## Method

1. Pressure cook beef until tender.
2. Reserve stock.
3. Prepare onion masala.
4. Add beef.
5. Mix rice.
6. Pour reserved stock.
7. Pressure cook one whistle.
8. Rest before serving.

# Palak Pulao

## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati rice
- 2 cups spinach
- Onion
- Tomato
- Ginger-garlic
- Green chili
- Cumin
- Garam masala
- Salt

Optional

- Peas
- Carrot
- Beans
- Paneer
- Cashews

## Method

1. Blend blanched spinach.
2. Cook onion masala.
3. Add spinach puree.
4. Add soaked rice.
5. Pour water.
6. Pressure cook one whistle.
7. Rest 10 minutes.

Serve with

- Plain curd
- Boondi raita
- Pickle
- Papad

# Pressure Cooker Tips

## Rice-to-Water Ratio

| Rice | Water |
|------|-------|
| 1 cup Basmati | 1½ cups (pressure cooker) |
| 1 cup Basmati | 1¾ cups (pot) |

---

## Resting Time

Always allow natural pressure release for 10 minutes.

This keeps rice fluffy and prevents grains from breaking.

# Meal Planning Tips

- Wash and chop vegetables on weekends.
- Marinate chicken overnight.
- Keep ginger-garlic paste ready.
- Soak rice for 20–30 minutes before cooking.
- Store fried onions in an airtight container.
- Freeze homemade stock for richer pulao and biryani.
