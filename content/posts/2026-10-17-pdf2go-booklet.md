# How to Convert a PDF to a Booklet and Print It

Printing a PDF as a booklet requires rearranging pages so that, when printed on both sides of paper and folded in half, the pages appear in the correct reading order. This process is called imposition. Below is a complete walkthrough using the online tool shown in your screenshot, along with alternative methods.

## Using pdf2go.com (Online Method)

The website visible in your screenshot, **pdf2go.com**, offers a free online booklet maker. Here is how to use it.

### Step 1: Upload Your PDF

1. Navigate to `https://www.pdf2go.com/create-booklet`.
2. Click the **Choose file** button or drag and drop your PDF into the red upload area.
3. Wait for the file to finish uploading.

### Step 2: Configure Booklet Settings

Expand the **Booklet settings** panel and adjust the following options:

- **Reading direction**: Select *Left to right* (standard for English) or *Right to left* (for Arabic, Hebrew, etc.).
- **Paper size**: Choose the size of paper you will print on (e.g., A4, Letter). The booklet pages will be half this size.
- **Orientation**: Set to *Landscape* so each sheet holds two booklet pages side by side.
- **Duplex flipping**: Select how your printer flips the paper — *Flip on long edge* is standard for landscape booklets.
- **Margins**: Set outer and inner (gutter) margins. The inner margin should be slightly larger to account for the fold.

### Step 3: Generate and Download

1. Click the blue **START** button.
2. Wait for the processing to complete (the timer in your screenshot shows 30 seconds as the limit).
3. Download the rearranged PDF.

### Step 4: Print the Booklet

1. Open the downloaded PDF in your PDF viewer.
2. Go to **File > Print**.
3. Select **Print on both sides** (duplex) with *Flip on long edge*.
4. Print all pages.
5. Collect the sheets, fold the entire stack in half, and staple along the spine.

## Using Adobe Acrobat Reader (Desktop Method)

Adobe Acrobat Reader has a built-in booklet printing feature that works well for smaller documents.

1. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader.
2. Go to **File > Print**.
3. Under **Page Sizing and Handling**, click **Booklet**.
4. Set **Booklet subset** to *Both sides*.
5. Set **Binding** to *Left* (or *Right* for right-to-left languages).
6. Adjust margins if needed.
7. Click **Print** and ensure your printer is set to duplex (double-sided) printing.

## Using LibreOffice Draw (Free Desktop Alternative)

LibreOffice Draw can impose booklet layouts without uploading files to the internet.

1. Open LibreOffice Draw.
2. Go to **File > Page Style** and set the page size to half your target paper size (e.g., A5 if printing on A4).
3. Insert your PDF pages using **Insert > Image** or by importing the PDF.
4. Manually arrange pages in booklet order, or use the **Print** dialog and select the **Brochure** option under page layout.
5. Print double-sided.

## Key Considerations

- **Page count**: Booklets work best with page counts that are multiples of 4 (4, 8, 12, 16, etc.). If your PDF has a page count that is not a multiple of 4, blank pages will be added automatically.
- **Paper weight**: Use slightly heavier paper (100 gsm or more) for a sturdier booklet.
- **Stapling**: For booklets up to about 64 pages, a long-reach stapler works well. Thicker booklets may require saddle-stitch binding or professional binding.
- **Test print**: Always print a single copy on plain paper first to verify page order and margins before using your final paper stock.
